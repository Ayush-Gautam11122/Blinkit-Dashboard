# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Blinkit Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using **Power BI** to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and outlet efficiency. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and KPIs, helping stakeholders monitor business performance and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance across Blinkit outlets.
- Track important business KPIs in real time.
- Compare sales across outlet types and locations.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Enable interactive filtering for deeper business analysis.

## 📈 Business Insights

- Supermarket Type outlets generated the highest sales.
- Regular Fat products contributed more revenue than Low Fat products.
- Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods were among the top-selling categories.
- Medium-sized outlets produced the highest overall sales.
- Tier 3 locations generated strong sales performance compared to other regions.
- Average customer ratings remained consistently high across outlet types.
- The dashboard enables quick identification of high-performing products and outlet segments.
